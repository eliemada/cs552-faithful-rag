\begin{abstract}
Faithfulness in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is typically measured by end-to-end answer-level metrics that conflate retrieval quality, grounded generation, and a miscalibrated judge into one number. We disentangle these axes on a span-grounded benchmark of 93 atomic claims (88 answerable queries) over intellectual-property and innovation-policy papers, and run four interventions: a sixteen-configuration retrieval ablation plus a nine-variant chunker-strategy sweep, a faithfulness verifier that runs DeBERTa-v3 NLI and an LLM judge side by side and is itself validated against a human-rated subset, a chunked-RAG vs.\ 1M-token long-context comparison with USD-normalised cost, and a Corrective RAG router \citep{yan2024corrective} with an abstain fallback. Headline numbers (all at $n{=}88$): an 800-char no-overlap chunker lifts E5-large MRR from 0.865 to \textbf{0.902} ($+3.7$\,pp) and nDCG@10 from 0.882 to 0.895, closing the M2 multi-hop gap; NLI and LLM-judge disagree by 55--61\,pp with \emph{zero} claims where both call \texttt{not\_supported}; a blinded 70-claim two-rater human eval pins the judge at Cohen's $\kappa{=}0.882$ vs.\ humans and NLI at $\kappa{=}-0.454$, conclusively ruling NLI out as a faithfulness gate; CRAG bins the M3 full $n{=}88$ gold into $\{85$ correct, $9$ ambiguous, $3$ incorrect$\}$ at the latency-optimal $(\tau_h{=}0.7, \tau_l{=}0.4)$ cell, capping the refinement upside at $+9.3$\,pp over the $87.6\%$ direct-correct baseline and catching $1/9$ unanswerables on abstain; at the full $n{=}88$ evaluable gold under a model-controlled three-arm RAGAS ablation (Gemini both sides) cross-validated under two judges (Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 4.6), long-context wins \texttt{faithfulness} ($+3.7$\,pp Sonnet, $+11.4$\,pp Haiku) and \texttt{context\_precision} ($+4.6$/$+6.7$\,pp) across both judges at $13\times$ the answer-LLM cost; \texttt{answer\_relevancy} is judge-sensitive (Haiku shows RAG $+9.4$\,pp; Sonnet shows the metric tied).
\end{abstract}


\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is deployed under the working assumption that retrieved context grounds the model's answer, yet evaluation is almost exclusively at the answer surface --- BLEU, ROUGE, or an LLM judge scores a generation against a reference and a single number is reported \citep{retrievalaugmentedgeneration,gao2024retrievalaugmented}. This collapses three failure modes --- bad retrieval, ungrounded generation despite good retrieval, and a miscalibrated judge --- into one metric, which is why a system can post a strong score while producing fabricated citations \citep{gao2023enabling,min-etal-2023-factscore}. The problem is acute in citation-heavy domains (law, policy, scientific review) where a misattributed span \emph{is} the harm.

The standard fix --- bolt on an NLI faithfulness check --- inherits NLI's known failure mode on long-form multi-claim outputs: trained on three-way premise/hypothesis pairs, NLI heads over-call \texttt{entailed} on retrieved passages \citep{min-etal-2023-factscore}. End-to-end RAG benchmarks \citep{es2024ragas,saadfalcon2024ares} compute faithfulness on model output but never validate the judge against a human reader. The stack reports high faithfulness numbers without a grounded notion of \emph{what faithful means}.

We disentangle these axes on a span-grounded benchmark of 93 claim-level QA pairs over IP/innovation-policy papers in which every claim carries byte-validated character spans into its source document, and run four targeted interventions: (i) a sixteen-configuration retrieval ablation plus a chunker-strategy sweep; (ii) a faithfulness verifier that runs DeBERTa-v3 NLI \citep{he2021debertav3} and an LLM judge side by side and validates both against a human-rated subset; (iii) a chunked-RAG vs.\ 1M-token long-context comparison with USD-normalised cost; (iv) a Corrective RAG router \citep{yan2024corrective} with an abstain fallback wired to the unanswerable stratum.

\paragraph{Contributions.} (1)~A character-span-grounded benchmark of 93 atomic claims (88 answerable + 9 unanswerable) with an IAA subset and six adversarial controls. (2)~A two-stage retrieval study: an embedder grid (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:retrieval-app}) names E5-large \citep{wang2024e5} coarse$+$rerank the M2 SOTA; a nine-variant chunker ablation lifts it to \textbf{0.902 MRR} ($+3.7$\,pp) with 800-char no-overlap chunks, identifying chunker geometry as a more tractable lever than embedder family for closing the multi-hop gap. (3)~A 55--61\,pp NLI-vs-judge disagreement together with a blinded 70-claim two-rater human eval that pins the judge at Cohen's $\kappa{=}0.882$ and NLI at $\kappa{=}-0.454$ \citep{cohen1960kappa} --- conclusively ruling out NLI as a faithfulness gate. (4)~A CRAG ablation on the full $n{=}88$ gold partitioning retrievals into $\{85, 9, 3\}$ at the latency-optimal cell, with a documented INCORRECT-branch abstain whose recall (1/9 unanswerables caught) bounds the cross-encoder's value as a corpus-faithfulness gate.


\section{Approach}
\label{sec:approach}

Four components, each owned by one team member: gold construction (group), retrieval (Elie), faithfulness verification (Andrea, Yusif co-rating), Corrective-RAG router (Faruk), long-context / RAGAS baseline (Yusif). All share one ingestion path: papers are LF-normalised to \texttt{document.md}, chunked by a configurable splitter, and indexed under a single \texttt{HybridRetriever} interface so an embedder, chunker, or reranker swap is a one-line config change.

\textbf{Gold benchmark.} 93 claim-level QA pairs (88 answerable + 9 unanswerable) over 36 IP/innovation-policy papers. Each pair carries atomic \texttt{claims} with byte-validated \texttt{supporting\_spans} (char offsets into LF-normalised \texttt{document.md}), plus \texttt{difficulty} and \texttt{category} tags. A schema validator re-resolves every span on every commit. A 36-pair IAA subset is double-reviewed for Cohen's $\kappa$; six adversarial controls (paraphrase, distractor, span-shift) break the single-class agreement paradox from the first IAA round.

\textbf{Retrieval pipeline.} A thin orchestrator over four dense backbones --- OpenAI \texttt{text-embedding-3-small}, BGE-M3 \citep{chen2024bgem3}, E5-large \citep{wang2024e5}, ColBERTv2 \citep{santhanam2022colbertv2} --- with an optional ZeroEntropy cross-encoder reranker on top-50. Documents split at coarse ($\sim$2k\,chars) and fine ($\sim$300\,chars), re-split for M3 at $\{200, 400, 600, 800\}$\,chars with $\{0\%, 50\%\}$ overlap plus a LangChain-style recursive variant. All variants share the gold-span resolver so paper-level and chunk-level metrics are directly comparable.

\textbf{Faithfulness verification.} Each claim is independently labelled by two heads. NLI encodes (\texttt{supporting\_quote}, \texttt{claim}) through DeBERTa-v3-large-mnli \citep{he2021debertav3}, mapping \texttt{entailment}~$\to$~\texttt{supported}; the LLM judge re-prompts a separate LLM with the same pair plus a literal-licence rubric. We deliberately do not fuse the heads --- the disagreement is the diagnostic. A blinded 70-claim two-rater human eval stratified across disagreement classes reports Cohen's $\kappa$ for both pairs.

\textbf{Corrective RAG router.} A cross-encoder \citep{yan2024corrective} scores top-$k$ retrievals and bins into CORRECT (score${\ge}\tau_h$), AMBIGUOUS ($\tau_l{\le}$\,score${<}\tau_h$), or INCORRECT (score${<}\tau_l$). CORRECT passes through; AMBIGUOUS triggers one LLM-driven query refinement and re-retrieval; INCORRECT is concretised as \emph{abstain}: \texttt{final\_documents=[]} and \texttt{abstained=True} propagate to the generator, which emits an explicit ``insufficient evidence'' string rather than hallucinating from a known-bad context. Abstain over off-corpus web fallback because the project's framing is faithfulness \emph{to a specific corpus} and is testable against the unanswerable gold stratum, whereas web fallback would change the threat model (\S\ref{sec:ethics}).


\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\textbf{Data.} 36 open-access IP/patent/policy papers from arXiv/OpenAlex, converted to LF-normalised \texttt{document.md} via Docling (gold spans are byte-offsets; CRLF artefacts would silently break resolution). Each author drafts QA pairs from a paper subset (28 Elie, 23 each Andrea/Faruk/Yusif); a second author reviews each pair. Six adversarial controls are seeded separately. Corpus is restricted to research-redistribution-licensed publications (\S\ref{sec:ethics}). \textbf{Evaluation.} Retrieval: hit@$\{1,5,10\}$, MRR, nDCG (paper-level); chunk-recall@10 (chunk-level). Faithfulness: per-claim binary label from NLI, LLM judge, and human; Cohen's $\kappa$ pairwise. End-to-end: four RAGAS metrics \citep{es2024ragas} on chunked-RAG vs.\ a Gemini~2.5~Flash 1M-token long-context arm, plus USD/query. CRAG: branch distribution at $(\tau_h,\tau_l)$; pre/post correct-retrieval; abstain-precision/recall on the unanswerable stratum; latency. \textbf{Setup.} M2 on EPFL RCP; M3 chunker ablation on SCITAS \texttt{izar} V100 (RCP saturated at $75/75$). Nine FAISS indexes built in $\sim$8\,h on one V100-PCIE-32GB. Generators: \texttt{gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18}, \texttt{claude-3-5-haiku-2024-10-22}, \texttt{deepseek-chat-v3}; judges: \texttt{gpt-4o-mini} (faithfulness), \texttt{claude-haiku-4-5} (RAGAS); all temperature~0, seed \texttt{2026}. Versions pinned in \texttt{uv.lock}.


\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Retrieval ablation}
\label{sec:retrieval}

The M2 sixteen-config grid (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:retrieval-app}) names E5-large \citep{wang2024e5} coarse$+$rerank the SOTA (MRR\,=\,0.878), beating closed-source OpenAI by $+8.6$\,pp MRR and ColBERTv2 \citep{santhanam2022colbertv2} by 17\,pp; multi-hop hit@10 collapses from $1.000$ on coarse to $0.667$--$0.833$ on fine, so the chunker is the dominant residual bottleneck. M3 sweeps nine chunker variants on top of that anchor: fixed-size windows at $\{200, 400, 600, 800\}$\,chars with $\{0\%, 50\%\}$ overlap plus a LangChain-style \texttt{recursive\_400}, all re-running the same 88 answerable queries; the M2 baseline is re-evaluated at $n{=}88$ for apples-to-apples comparison.

\texttt{s800\_o0} is the new SOTA at \textbf{0.902 MRR} ($+3.7$\,pp) and \textbf{0.895 nDCG@10} ($+1.3$\,pp); \texttt{s600\_o0} and \texttt{s800\_o400} push hit@10 to $0.989$ ($+1.2$\,pp). No-overlap beats $50\%$-overlap on MRR (\texttt{s400\_o0}~>~\texttt{s400\_o200} by $+6.5$\,pp; \texttt{s800\_o0}~>~\texttt{s800\_o400} by $+4.0$\,pp); \texttt{recursive\_400} underperforms every fixed-size variant by fragmenting sentence-level evidence the reranker scores.

\subsection{Faithfulness verification}
\label{sec:faith}

On 24 answerable gold questions closed-book from three generators, NLI marks 18/24 ($0.750$) faithful at every provider while the LLM judge marks $0.138$/$0.173$/$0.198$ --- a 55--61\,pp gap matching a known NLI failure mode \citep{min-etal-2023-factscore,gao2023enabling}. A blinded 70-claim two-rater pass yields majority-human-vs-judge \textbf{$\kappa{=}0.882$} ($n{=}56$), majority-human-vs-NLI $\kappa{=}-0.454$ ($n{=}56$), inter-rater $\kappa{=}0.583$ ($n{=}70$) \citep{cohen1960kappa}: the judge tracks human reading; NLI is below chance on the disagreement strata. On \texttt{nli\_sup\_judge\_not} ($n{=}21$), human matches judge 18/21 (86\%); on \texttt{nli\_not\_judge\_sup} ($n{=}17$), 17/17 (100\%). \textbf{The LLM judge is the trustworthy head; NLI should not be used as a faithfulness gate.}

\subsection{End-to-end RAG vs long-context}
\label{sec:ragas}

M3 scales the M2 preliminary ($n{=}8$) to the full $n{=}88$ evaluable gold (the 9 unanswerable and 6 adversarial pairs have no reference answer for RAGAS to score) under a three-arm ablation disentangling two confounds (long context vs.\ chunked retrieval; Gemini vs.\ GPT). All RAG arms use the M3 chunker-SOTA retriever \texttt{e5\_rerank\_s800\_o0}; long-context loads cited \texttt{document.md} into Gemini~2.5~Flash's 1M-token window. We run the full sweep under two judges (\texttt{claude-haiku-4-5} and \texttt{claude-sonnet-4-6}) to bound judge sensitivity; per-judge tables in Appendix~\ref{tab:ragas-app}. \textbf{Direction-stable across judges}: model-controlled (both Gemini), LC wins \texttt{faithfulness} ($+3.7$\,pp Sonnet, $+11.4$\,pp Haiku) and \texttt{context\_precision} ($+4.6$/$+6.7$\,pp); the direction holds, the magnitude is judge-dependent. \textbf{Judge-sensitive}: under Haiku, RAG-Gemini beats LC on \texttt{answer\_relevancy} by $+9.4$\,pp; under Sonnet the gap closes to LC $+1.1$\,pp --- a Haiku-specific finding, consistent with RAGAS's documented judge sensitivity on this metric \citep{es2024ragas}. The "answer\_relevancy flip" therefore does not survive a stronger judge and is reported as a sensitivity result rather than a headline conclusion. LC costs \$0.00370/query, $13\times$ the cheapest RAG arm (\$0.00029); chunked RAG remains Pareto-optimal on cost, LC wins the grounded-faithfulness and context-precision axes consistently across judges.

\paragraph{Corrective RAG.}\label{sec:crag} M3 scales CRAG \citep{yan2024corrective} from the M2 10-question slice to the full $n{=}88$ gold (97 with unanswerables). The probe (\texttt{e5\_large\_coarse\_rerank}, $k{=}10$, cross-encoder \texttt{ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2}) bins each retrieval analytically over the $(\tau_h, \tau_l)$ grid \citep{yan2024corrective}; at the latency-optimal cell $(\tau_h{=}0.7, \tau_l{=}0.4)$, branches partition as $\{$correct: $85$, ambiguous: $9$, incorrect: $3\}$. With direct-correct retrieval already at $87.6\%$ ($85/97$) the refinement upside is bounded at $+9.3$\,pp ($9$ ambiguous queries rescuable in one round); the INCORRECT branch abstains (\S\ref{sec:approach}) and catches $1/9$ unanswerables at $\tau_l{=}0.4$ (precision $0.33$, recall $0.11$) --- the cross-encoder is a confident-but-shallow abstain detector that scores $7/9$ unanswerables above $\tau_h$ because lexical overlap with retrieved chunks masquerades as evidence, a known failure mode that motivates the corpus-rather-than-web framing (\S\ref{sec:ethics}). Sweeping $\tau_h \in \{0.6, 0.7, 0.8\}$ shifts at most $3$ borderline queries between CORRECT and AMBIGUOUS; $\tau_l$ is the operative knob.


\section{Ethical considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

The benchmark is English IP/policy text only and Anglo-American-biased; deployment elsewhere needs a multilingual embedder (BGE-M3 supports this), language-native NLI, and re-annotation; signed-language extension needs an upstream sign-to-text recogniser and re-validation. Beneficiaries are reviewers in citation-heavy workflows where misattribution is the harm; the principal risk is the inverse --- a silent faithfulness gate launders fabrications behind a green-light score, which is why we validate NLI/judge against humans and why the CRAG INCORRECT branch abstains. The judge is closed-weight; the two-rater eval is a lower bound. Off-corpus web fallback was rejected because it changes the threat model from corpus to open-web faithfulness.


\paragraph{Conclusion.}\label{sec:conclusion} Four interventions on one span-grounded benchmark pin component-level failure modes end-to-end metrics blur. A chunker ablation lifts M2 to \textbf{0.902 MRR} / \textbf{0.989 hit@10} (800-char no-overlap); a 70-claim blinded eval pins the LLM judge at $\kappa{=}0.882$ and NLI at $\kappa{=}-0.454$, ruling NLI out as a gate; CRAG on full $n{=}88$ partitions retrievals into $\{85, 9, 3\}$ at the latency-optimal cell with a documented abstain branch bounded at $1/9$ unanswerable recall; long-context wins RAGAS \texttt{faithfulness} ($+3.7$ Sonnet / $+11.4$\,pp Haiku model-controlled) and \texttt{context\_precision} across both judges at $13\times$ the answer-LLM cost; \texttt{answer\_relevancy} is judge-sensitive. \textbf{Limitations:} English corpus, two raters, closed judge, cross-encoder abstain detector overcalls unanswerables ($7/9$ above $\tau_h$). \textbf{Future work:} multilingual port, IAA closure, hierarchical chunker, per-domain abstain threshold.
