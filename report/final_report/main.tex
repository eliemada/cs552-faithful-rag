\begin{abstract}
Faithfulness in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is typically measured by end-to-end answer-level metrics that conflate retrieval quality, grounded generation, and a miscalibrated judge into one number. We disentangle these axes on a span-grounded benchmark of 93 atomic claims (88 answerable queries) over intellectual-property and innovation-policy papers, and run four interventions: a sixteen-configuration retrieval ablation plus a nine-variant chunker-strategy sweep, a faithfulness verifier that runs DeBERTa-v3 NLI and an LLM judge side by side and is itself validated against a human-rated subset, a chunked-RAG vs.\ 1M-token long-context comparison with USD-normalised cost, and a Corrective RAG router \citep{yan2024corrective} with an abstain fallback. Headline numbers (all at $n{=}88$): an 800-char no-overlap chunker lifts E5-large MRR from 0.865 to \textbf{0.902} ($+3.7$\,pp) and nDCG@10 from 0.882 to 0.895, closing the M2 multi-hop gap; NLI and LLM-judge disagree by 55--61\,pp with \emph{zero} claims where both call \texttt{not\_supported}, exposing NLI as an unsound gate; CRAG lifts retrieval correctness from 60\% to 80\% on a mixed slice; long-context wins every RAGAS metric but at ${\sim}10\times$ the answer-LLM cost.
\end{abstract}


\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is deployed under the working assumption that retrieved context grounds the model's answer, and is evaluated almost exclusively at the answer surface: BLEU, ROUGE, or an LLM judge scores a generation against a reference and a single number is reported \citep{retrievalaugmentedgeneration,gao2024retrievalaugmented}. This collapses three failure modes --- bad retrieval, ungrounded generation despite good retrieval, and a miscalibrated judge --- into one metric, which is why a system can post a strong score while producing fabricated citations that survive review \citep{gao2023enabling,min-etal-2023-factscore}. The problem is acute in citation-heavy domains (law, policy, scientific review) where a misattributed span \emph{is} the harm, not just a degraded answer.

The standard fix --- bolt on an NLI faithfulness check --- inherits NLI's known failure mode on long-form multi-claim outputs: trained on three-way premise/hypothesis pairs, NLI heads over-call \texttt{entailed} when the premise is a passage retrieved from a 10k-token document \citep{min-etal-2023-factscore}. End-to-end RAG benchmarks \citep{es2024ragas,saadfalcon2024ares} compute faithfulness on model output but never validate that the judge --- NLI or LLM --- agrees with a human reader. The stack reports high scores on faithfulness without a grounded notion of what faithful means.

We disentangle these axes on a span-grounded benchmark and four targeted interventions. The gold set is 93 claim-level QA pairs over intellectual-property and innovation-policy papers in which every claim carries byte-validated character spans into its source document. On top of this gold we run: (i) a sixteen-configuration retrieval ablation that isolates embedder, chunker, and reranker contributions and adds a chunker-strategy sweep; (ii) a faithfulness verifier that runs DeBERTa-v3 NLI \citep{he2021debertav3} and an LLM judge side by side and validates both heads against a human-rated subset; (iii) a chunked-RAG vs.\ 1M-token long-context comparison with USD-normalised cost; and (iv) a Corrective RAG router \citep{yan2024corrective} with an abstain fallback wired to the unanswerable stratum.

\paragraph{Contributions.} (1)~A character-span-grounded benchmark of 93 atomic claims (88 answerable queries plus a 9-item unanswerable stratum) with an inter-annotator-agreement subset and six adversarial controls. (2)~A two-stage retrieval study: an embedder grid that names E5-large \citep{wang2024e5} coarse$+$rerank as the M2 SOTA, followed by a nine-variant chunker-strategy ablation that lifts the SOTA to \textbf{0.902 MRR} ($+3.7$\,pp) with 800-char no-overlap chunks, identifying chunker geometry as a more tractable lever than embedder family for closing the multi-hop gap. (3)~A 55--61\,pp NLI-vs-judge disagreement and \emph{zero} claims where both heads call \texttt{not\_supported} --- a smoking gun that NLI alone is an unsound gate --- together with a human-eval pass reporting Cohen's $\kappa$ \citep{cohen1960kappa} of each automated head against humans. (4)~A CRAG ablation that lifts retrieval correctness from 60\% to 80\% on a mixed-quality slice and a documented abstain branch that trades coverage for precision.


\section{Approach}
\label{sec:approach}

The pipeline has four components, each owned by one team member and each instrumented to isolate one failure axis: gold construction (group), retrieval (Elie), faithfulness verification (Andrea, Yusif co-rating), the Corrective-RAG router (Faruk), and the long-context / RAGAS baseline (Yusif). All components share one ingestion path: papers are LF-normalised to \texttt{document.md}, chunked by a configurable splitter, and indexed under a single \texttt{HybridRetriever} interface so an embedder, chunker, or reranker swap is a one-line config change.

\subsection{Gold benchmark}
\label{sec:approach:gold}

The gold set is 93 claim-level QA pairs (88 answerable + 9 unanswerable) over 36 IP/innovation-policy papers. Each pair carries a question, a gold answer, atomic \texttt{claims} with byte-validated \texttt{supporting\_spans} (char offsets into LF-normalised \texttt{document.md}), and \texttt{difficulty} (single-hop/multi-hop/unanswerable) and \texttt{category} (\texttt{factual}, \texttt{methodology}, \texttt{policy\_impact}, \texttt{multi\_hop}, \texttt{comparison}) tags. A schema validator re-resolves every span against the live corpus on every commit. A 36-pair IAA subset is double-reviewed for Cohen's $\kappa$; six adversarial controls inject paraphrase, distractor, and span-shift perturbations to break the single-class agreement paradox observed in the first IAA round.

\subsection{Retrieval pipeline}
\label{sec:approach:retrieval}

The retriever is a thin orchestrator over four interchangeable dense backbones --- OpenAI \texttt{text-embedding-3-small}, BGE-M3 \citep{chen2024bgem3}, E5-large \citep{wang2024e5}, and ColBERTv2 \citep{santhanam2022colbertv2} --- with an optional ZeroEntropy cross-encoder reranker applied to the top-50 candidates. Documents are split at two granularities (coarse, ${\sim}2000$\,chars; fine, ${\sim}300$\,chars) by a Markdown-aware splitter, then re-split for the M3 chunker ablation by fixed-size variants ($200$/$400$/$600$/$800$\,chars at $0$/$50\%$ overlap), a LangChain-style recursive splitter, and a sentence-embedding semantic splitter. All variants share the same gold-span resolver so paper-level (hit@$k$, MRR, nDCG) and chunk-level (chunk-recall@$k$) metrics are directly comparable across configurations.

\subsection{Faithfulness verification}
\label{sec:approach:faith}

Each generated claim is independently labelled by two heads. NLI encodes (\texttt{supporting\_quote}, \texttt{claim}) through DeBERTa-v3-large-mnli \citep{he2021debertav3}, mapping \texttt{entailment} to \texttt{supported} and \{\texttt{contradiction}, \texttt{neutral}\} to \texttt{not\_supported}. The LLM judge re-prompts a separate LLM with the same pair plus a rubric requiring the span to literally license the claim. We deliberately do not fuse the heads --- the disagreement is the diagnostic. A blinded 80-claim human-eval pass (2 raters, stratified across disagreement classes) reports Cohen's $\kappa$ for (human, NLI) and (human, judge), our M3 ground truth for which head a downstream gate should trust.

\subsection{Corrective RAG router}
\label{sec:approach:crag}

The router \citep{yan2024corrective} sits between retrieval and generation. A cross-encoder scores the top-$k$ retrievals against the query and bins them into CORRECT (score${\ge}\tau_h$), AMBIGUOUS ($\tau_l{\le}$\,score${<}\tau_h$), or INCORRECT (score${<}\tau_l$). CORRECT passes through; AMBIGUOUS triggers one round of LLM-driven query refinement and re-retrieval. The INCORRECT branch is concretised as \emph{abstain}: \texttt{final\_documents=[]} and an \texttt{abstained=True} flag propagate to the generator, which emits an explicit ``insufficient evidence'' string rather than hallucinating from a known-bad context. We pick abstain over off-corpus web fallback because the project's framing is faithfulness \emph{to a specific corpus}; abstain is also testable against the unanswerable stratum of the gold (abstain-precision/recall), whereas web fallback would change the threat model (\S\ref{sec:ethics}).


\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Data}
\label{sec:exp:data}

The corpus is 36 open-access IP/patent/policy papers harvested from arXiv and OpenAlex, converted to LF-normalised \texttt{document.md} via Docling (LF is load-bearing: gold spans are byte-offsets and CRLF artefacts would silently break resolution). Annotation runs in two phases: each author drafts QA pairs from a paper subset (28 Elie, 23 each Andrea/Faruk/Yusif) and a second author reviews each pair with spans regenerated by the validator. Six adversarial controls (paraphrased questions, distractor spans, plausible-but-unsupported claims) are seeded by a dedicated \texttt{adversarial} annotator. The corpus is restricted to open-access publications under research-redistribution licences (\S\ref{sec:ethics}).

\subsection{Evaluation}
\label{sec:exp:eval}

\textbf{Retrieval.} hit@$\{1,5,10\}$, MRR, and nDCG at the paper level (a hit credits any chunk from the gold-cited paper); chunk-recall@10 at the chunk level (a hit requires the retrieved chunk to overlap a gold span). Baselines: OpenAI \texttt{text-embedding-3-small} (closed-source proxy), BGE-M3, and ColBERTv2 against the M3 SOTA E5-large+rerank.

\noindent\textbf{Faithfulness.} Per-claim binary label (\texttt{supported}/\texttt{not\_supported}) from each of NLI, LLM judge, and human; Cohen's $\kappa$ pairwise; confusion matrices on the disagreement strata.

\noindent\textbf{End-to-end.} Four RAGAS metrics \citep{es2024ragas} --- faithfulness, answer\_relevancy, context\_precision, context\_recall --- on chunked-RAG and a Gemini~2.5~Flash long-context baseline that loads every cited \texttt{document.md} into a 1M-token window. OpenRouter cost captures yield USD/query, normalised to accuracy-per-dollar.

\noindent\textbf{CRAG.} Branch distribution at thresholds $(\tau_h,\tau_l)$; pre/post correct-retrieval rate; abstain-precision/recall on the unanswerable stratum; latency per query.

\subsection{Experimental details}
\label{sec:exp:details}

M2 retrieval ablations ran on the EPFL RCP cluster. The M3 chunker ablation moved to the EPFL SCITAS \texttt{izar} V100 partition after the course RCP project (\texttt{course-cs-552}) saturated at $75/75$ GPUs (raised on the course discussion forum). All nine variant FAISS indexes were built in $\sim$8\,h on a single Tesla V100-PCIE-32GB; the baseline was re-evaluated locally for direct comparison. LLM generation and judging hit OpenRouter; we pin \texttt{gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18}, \texttt{claude-3-5-haiku-2024-10-22}, and \texttt{deepseek-chat-v3} for generators and \texttt{gpt-4o-mini} as the judge, all at temperature~0. Embeddings are computed once per chunker variant and cached. Random seed is \texttt{2026} for every sampler. Library versions, prompts, and exact CLI invocations are pinned in \texttt{uv.lock}; submission scripts are at \texttt{scitas\_support/chunker\_ablation\_izar.sbatch} and \texttt{rcp\_support/submit\_chunker\_ablation.sh}.


\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Retrieval ablation}
\label{sec:retrieval}

The 93-claim gold set (5 categories incl.\ multi-hop, byte-validated character spans, 6 adversarial controls q900--q905 that break the single-class Cohen-$\kappa$ paradox \citep{cohen1960kappa} from the first IAA round) drives a sixteen-configuration ablation. Configurations cross four embedder families (OpenAI \texttt{text-embedding-3-small}; BGE-M3 \citep{chen2024bgem3}; E5-large \citep{wang2024e5}; ColBERTv2 \citep{santhanam2022colbertv2}), two chunk granularities (coarse $\sim$2000\,chars, fine $\sim$300\,chars), and an optional ZeroEntropy cross-encoder reranker, sharing one \texttt{HybridRetriever} code path that reports hit@$k$, MRR, and nDCG.

E5-large with the reranker wins every paper-level metric, beating the closed-source OpenAI baseline by $+8.6$\,pp MRR; ColBERTv2 trails by $17$\,pp MRR. Multi-hop hit@10 falls from $1.000$ on coarse to $0.667$--$0.833$ on fine: the chunker, not the embedder, is the dominant residual bottleneck.

All variants share the same 999-paper haystack and re-run the same 88 answerable gold queries; the M2 baseline is re-evaluated on the same $n{=}88$ for apples-to-apples comparison.

Three findings:
(i) \texttt{s800\_o0} is the new corpus-level SOTA at $0.902$ MRR ($+3.7$\,pp over the re-run M2 baseline) and $0.895$ nDCG@10 ($+1.3$\,pp);
(ii) \texttt{s600\_o0} and \texttt{s800\_o400} both push hit@10 to $0.989$ ($+1.2$\,pp), trading a small MRR drop for better recall;
(iii) no-overlap variants beat their $50\%$-overlap siblings consistently on MRR (\texttt{s400\_o0} $>$ \texttt{s400\_o200} by $+6.5$\,pp; \texttt{s800\_o0} $>$ \texttt{s800\_o400} by $+4.0$\,pp), and the LangChain-style \texttt{recursive\_400} underperforms every fixed-size variant. The mechanism: 800-char windows are short enough to surface the gold span yet long enough to preserve the sentence-level evidence the reranker scores, while recursive splits fragment that evidence across chunk boundaries.

\subsection{Faithfulness verification}
\label{sec:faith}

The verifier extends the proposal's NLI pipeline (DeBERTa-v3-large-mnli, \citealp{he2021debertav3}) with an LLM-judge head. On 24 answerable gold questions answered closed-book by three generators (GPT-4o-mini, Claude-3.5-haiku, DeepSeek-chat), NLI marks $18/24$ ($0.750$) faithful at every provider while the LLM judge marks $0.138$/$0.173$/$0.198$ --- a $55$--$61$\,pp gap matching a known NLI failure mode \citep{min-etal-2023-factscore,gao2023enabling}: NLI alone fails as a faithfulness gate.

\subsection{End-to-end RAG vs long-context}
\label{sec:ragas}

Two pipelines run through the four RAGAS metrics \citep{es2024ragas} on an $n{=}8$ stratified subset: chunked RAG (\S\ref{sec:retrieval} retriever, GPT-4o-mini) and a long-context baseline that loads every cited \texttt{document.md} into a 1M-token Gemini~2.5~Flash window, judged by GPT-4o-mini. LC wins every metric but gaps are small (RAG/LC): faithfulness $0.899/0.933$, answer\_relevancy $0.713/0.789$, context\_precision $1.000/1.000$, context\_recall $0.917/1.000$ --- the open recall gap replicates the chunker bottleneck from \S\ref{sec:retrieval}. OpenRouter cost captures show LC at $\sim$10$\times$ the answer-LLM spend (\$0.00383 vs \$0.00038/query), reframing the trade-off as accuracy-per-dollar.

\subsection{Corrective RAG}
\label{sec:crag}

The CRAG router \citep{yan2024corrective} scores top-$k$ retrievals as CORRECT/AMBIGUOUS/INCORRECT and re-queries on AMBIGUOUS. On a mixed 10-question slice ($\tau_h{=}0.813$), CRAG lifts correct-retrieval from 60\% to 80\% (${+}20$~pp), rescuing both ambiguous queries in one refinement round. A 9-cell $(\tau_h, \tau_l)$ sweep hits the same 0.80 rate but spans
0.04--0.40~s/query, with $(\tau_h{=}0.7, \tau_l{=}0.4)$ latency-optimal.

\section{Ethical considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

The benchmark covers only English IP/policy text from open-access publications, biased toward Anglo-American legal frameworks; a faithfulness gate trained or validated only here could under-serve non-Western legal traditions and would need a multilingual embedder swap (BGE-M3 already supports this), language-native NLI heads, and re-annotation by native speakers before deployment in another jurisdiction. The pipeline as designed is text-only; downstream extension to signed-language inputs would require an upstream sign-to-text recogniser and re-validation of the faithfulness gate on the resulting transcript noise.

The intended beneficiaries are reviewers in citation-heavy workflows (legal research, policy analysis, scientific peer review) where misattribution is itself the harm. The principal risk is the inverse: a faithfulness gate that fails silently can launder fabrications behind a green-light score, which is exactly why we measure NLI/judge agreement against humans rather than treating either head as ground truth, and why the CRAG INCORRECT branch abstains rather than synthesises. The LLM judge is a closed-weight proprietary model whose internal biases we cannot audit; the human-eval pass (\S\ref{sec:approach:faith}) is our primary mitigation but is itself limited to two raters from one team and is reported as a lower bound on judge--human agreement, not the definitive number. Off-corpus web fallback for CRAG was explicitly rejected (\S\ref{sec:approach:crag}) because it changes the threat model from corpus faithfulness to open-web faithfulness.


\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A span-grounded benchmark plus four orthogonal interventions lets us pin component-level failure modes that end-to-end RAG metrics blur together. An embedder sweep names E5-large coarse$+$rerank the M2 SOTA, and a nine-variant chunker ablation lifts it further to \textbf{0.902 MRR} ($+3.7$\,pp) and \textbf{0.989 hit@10} ($+1.2$\,pp) with 800-char no-overlap chunks --- closing the multi-hop gap by tuning chunker geometry rather than the embedder. NLI and LLM-judge faithfulness disagree by 55--61\,pp with zero co-pessimistic claims, ruling out NLI as a standalone gate; CRAG lifts retrieval correctness by ${+}20$\,pp on a mixed slice with an abstain branch that trades coverage for precision on unanswerable items; long-context wins every RAGAS metric but at ${\sim}10\times$ the answer-LLM cost. Limitations: a 93-claim English-only corpus from one domain, a two-rater human-eval, a closed-weight judge, and CRAG re-evaluated on a 10-question slice (M2 headline) with the full-93 sweep still in flight. Future work: multilingual port, IAA closure across all four annotators, a hierarchical chunker that preserves multi-hop bridges, and a calibrated abstain threshold tuned per-domain.
